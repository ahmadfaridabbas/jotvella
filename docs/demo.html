Jotvella — Product Vision

product direction

A personal knowledge space should feel less like managing a system and more like returning to a familiar desk. Jotvella brings the reassurance of notebooks together with the freedom to shape a page as ideas take form.

What we’re making

A quiet place to collect, organize, write, and revisit the knowledge that matters to one person. The structure should be there when you need it, then recede while you think.

Product goals

  • Make capture immediate, and make returning to an idea feel effortless.
  • Give every thought a natural home: notebook, section, page, or a freeform space.
  • Keep writing comfortable across a quick note and a long-form session.

Principles

The interface should make the user’s information feel more important than the interface itself.

Local-first by intention. Calm by default. Flexible without asking people to learn a new way to think.
